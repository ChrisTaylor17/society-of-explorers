## Project background

Society of Explorers is an online great-books salon club. Small groups read a public-domain text and meet on video; Mira Vale, an AI tutor (always labeled as AI), asks the questions. Thinker personas are public-domain figures only (for example Socrates, Plato, Aristotle, Marcus Aurelius, Augustine, Nietzsche) and are shown as AI interpretations, never as the real people.

Older plans for devices, tokens and on-chain payments were dropped; that code lives in `archive/`. See `CLAUDE.md` for the working rules. Keep responses plain modern English and tied to privacy and real human value.
