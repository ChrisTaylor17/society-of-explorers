## Objection Bank

### Is this an investment?

No. It is patronage. Patrons receive access, recognition, community artifacts, ritual priority, membership credit, and founding status. They are not receiving equity, debt, securities, dividends, or a promise of financial return.

### Is the Boston space secured?

No. The campaign should describe it only as the intended first physical node if lease status, repairs, access, and landlord alignment make the path viable.

### What happens if that location does not work?

Funds still support the same mission: the private AI network, encrypted personal pods, creator rituals, and future physical-node path. Public updates should explain the decision and next responsible use of funds.

### Why $120,000?

It is enough to prove serious patron demand, fund due diligence and buildout planning, support a window/founder-wall activation, and create operating runway while digital membership and project pledges grow.

### Why now?

AI is moving fast, but most products are optimized for extraction, dependency, and generic output. Society of Explorers is built around sovereignty, memory control, reflection, and community. The window gives that idea a real-world signal.
