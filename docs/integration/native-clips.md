# Clips in the phone app

Choose **Explore Clips** before connecting an account or from the verified member screen. Both paths open the same two short videos and a reading clip. The reading clip uses Philemon 1:3 from the publisher’s public-domain World English Bible Classic. Its button opens the complete Philemon study, including the salon guide. **Find an online Circle** opens public listings for guests and sets the Online filter for members. Guests can choose a Circle before signing in and open that Circle after member verification. [Publisher reuse terms](https://ebible.org/eng-web/webfaq.htm) · [Philemon study](root-kernel-philemon.md) · [Online Circle verification](native-online-circles-verification.json)

**How a Circle works** and **Who gets the night sky?** use the approved silent MP4s and exact transcripts. Each clip starts on a tap and can pause or replay. The Archivist Reel includes its credit in the picture. The card supplies the original source, CC BY 4.0 terms and edit disclosure. The displayed duration is approximate. Chromium reported 23.955 seconds for the Circle introduction and macOS reported 24.664 seconds. Both measured the Reel at 24 seconds. [Reel rights and edit](paranal-reel.md) · [ESO source](https://www.eso.org/public/videos/eso2607d/) · [ESO reuse terms](https://www.eso.org/public/outreach/copyright/)

The build checks the reviewed file hashes, transcripts and attribution before copying the two derivatives into platform resources. The player bridge accepts a clip ID, playback request key and play state. The Android view uses the app’s resources with VideoView. The iOS view uses the packaged files with AVPlayerLayer. Changing the clip, leaving the screen or losing app focus releases playback. Returning to the app requires another tap. The transcripts remain readable in builds that lack the installed player. [Android VideoView](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/widget/VideoView) · [Apple AVPlayerLayer](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfoundation/avplayerlayer)
