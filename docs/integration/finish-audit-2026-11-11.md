## Concrete local defects and unfinished surfaces

These findings come from baseline source. They should become bounded fixes, not another strategy cycle.

1. **Music waitlist gives false success.** [The client](../../app/music-therapy/page.tsx) sets `submitted=true` after any resolved fetch, including HTTP 500. [The endpoint](../../app/api/music-therapy/interest/route.ts) returns 500 on an unsuccessful insert. The client offers `muse/polar/both/learn`, but the endpoint accepts `learn/have_device/need_device/other`; three choices silently become `learn`. Require the exact success receipt, preserve the entry on failure, permit explicit retry and align the supported choices. No real submission is needed to test this.
2. **Travel presents fictional activity and no-op actions.** [Travel](../../app/travel/page.tsx) supplies three hard-coded people with biometric scores, historical passport stamps, Explorer #0042, 1,240 EXP and “VERIFIED ON BASE.” Its Travala, Dtravel and Resonant Match buttons perform no action. It also promises biometric check-in, tokens and scanning rewards without proof. Retain the vision as an honest non-bookable proposal and link to working readings/salons; remove simulated personal balances and offers.
3. **Member voice and mint controls pretend to act.** [Members](../../app/members/page.tsx) toggles a recording-looking state without recording; [Waddle](../../app/waddle/page.tsx) has an inert “MINT NFT (SOON)” button; [Twiddle detail](../../app/twiddle/[id]/page.tsx) has an enabled inert “MINT” button. Remove those controls while preserving actual text, voice and sharing behavior. Do not create token functionality merely to fill the gap.
4. **Space discovery equates errors with emptiness.** [World](../../app/world/page.tsx) initializes zero totals and accepts `d.spaces || []` without checking status. A network or API error can therefore appear as no scanned spaces. Distinguish verified empty, loading, unavailable and malformed results, with an explicit retry. Also correct “SHA-256 proves authenticity”: a digest checks byte identity, not a photo's truth or ownership.
5. **Two recovery links point at an absent route.** [Leaderboard](../../app/leaderboard/page.tsx) links twice to `/community`; the repo has no matching page, public file or Next redirect. Use `/explore`. The literal-link scan found no other missing internal href under its scope; computed links and external destinations were not accepted by this scan.
6. **Older informational claims drift from the product.** [Data layer](../../app/data-layer/page.tsx) directs `/join` visitors to “Mint your soulbound membership NFT,” describes the Boston venue as the first node and implies a current sovereign identity. The join page actually offers a free account and optional support. [Music](../../app/music-therapy/page.tsx) describes future EEG/HRV adaptation in present tense. Convert to clearly scoped proposals or remove unsupported claims from the trial.
7. **The legacy intensive Guide program is not finished.** [The existing writer audit](guide-program-writer-audit.md) records G01–G08/G10 still open: fresh Guide authority, partial writes, duplicate retries, graduation and schema compatibility. The current code is not a safe shortcut to four weekly Circles. Keep this program unavailable to trial participants until the transactional work and actual schema are accepted.

Literal “TODO” on the task board means a real task status. Input `placeholder` attributes are legitimate hints. Quest buttons wired by controller IDs are not missing handlers. Do not remove these by keyword. `fetchRituals()`'s mock fallback and `activateOnChain()` are retained stubs with no caller found by the baseline import/name scan; they are not demonstrated customer activity. `writePodCommitment()` **does** have a caller, `/api/movement/pod/refresh`, but always reports `wroteOnchain:false`; do not present its server-side draft as an on-chain commitment. [Source](../../lib/movement/commitment.ts). The Buck Holler persona carries an unresolved naming-clearance note and should remain outside a commercial launch claim.
