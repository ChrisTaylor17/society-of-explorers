## Reading in the room

Point at **Read selection** from recent discussion or the shared salon. The room checks current membership and loads the upcoming meeting's exact book, edition and assigned range. It supports the pinned Great Books editions and the two starter readings already bound to those editions. For other assignments, the regular Circle shows the reading plan.

Each text block is paged in order, preserving its source lines and words. A table, image or other unavailable text block has its own notice and stays in the sequence. **Source info** shows the edition credit, license and the scope of the U.S. rights review. Page turns within a block happen locally. Opening another block checks membership and the assignment again before requesting the public text. The passage request sends only the book, edition and block coordinates. If the assignment changes, return to the salon or discussion and choose the new selection.
