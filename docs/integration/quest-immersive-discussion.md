# Quest immersive Circle discussion

Open a Circle in Quest Browser and connect the existing account. A compatible browser offers **Enter immersive room**. Entry pauses the regular Circle view and clears its private content. The room opens with a generic panel. One controller trigger checks the existing account, and a second checks current Circle membership and loads recent discussion.

The room shows up to five visible contributions from the first archive page. The trigger advances to the next preview and checks for current discussion after the last one. Point the controller at **Write a post** or **Reply** and press the trigger. The panel keyboard supports up to 180 characters, including English letters, numbers and common punctuation. **Review** loads the exact text for confirmation, including the reply reference. Aim at **Post** and press the trigger to send. Repeating the trigger at the Review position cannot send. A saved post can be read in the same Circle on the regular web page.

The headset has its own session and uses the regular Circle's account checks, discussion endpoints and current membership rules. If delivery is uncertain, the panel lets a member check the receipt or retry the exact reviewed request. Account changes, headset focus loss, offline events, graphics failure, navigation and exit clear the private panel and draft and stop pending work. After an interrupted send, check the regular discussion before posting again. Use squeeze or the regular page's **Exit** button to leave, then check the member session again to resume in the browser. The full archive, long messages, other scripts, source readings, safety controls and live meeting links are in the regular browser. Members who enter through the shared salon pilot can open this discussion while keeping their checked-in seat. Back returns to the salon count. Live conversation uses the browser meeting link.
