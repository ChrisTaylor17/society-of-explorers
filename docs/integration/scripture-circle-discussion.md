# Scripture study discussion

Philemon and the Good Samaritan each offer a question and a link to its verse. In a checked web Circle, choose **Discuss this study** from the assigned reading or the Circle itself. Load the current archive, then choose **Use study question in a draft**. The phone reader takes members to the same archive and draft controls. On the final text page in the Quest room, **Use study question** checks the archive again before opening the composer.

The question and link are editable. Members use the existing preview and confirmation controls to post. The study URL provides public reading context, and the existing discussion routes authorize every archive read and post against current membership and Circle visibility. Web links keep the study through sign-in. A duplicate study parameter or one mixed with a Reel or a thread is refused.

## Local verification

- The compiled web flow created an online Circle, opened its assigned study and saved an edited draft after confirmation. A signed-in nonmember could read the public question and was refused the private archive and draft.
- Both phone simulations saved the exact edited question and verse link. A retained button from the reader could not replace the current draft.
- The compiled Quest room read the assignment, showed the same draft in its review panel and saved it through the regular discussion routes. A new draft was refused after the Circle became hidden.
- The 67-table PostgreSQL restore preserved the Gutenberg passage guard and detected replacement with the earlier validator. All eight indexed books kept their link and quote support.
