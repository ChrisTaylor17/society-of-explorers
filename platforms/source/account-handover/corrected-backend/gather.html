Quieter door: When does a ledger earn its keep? — bill pay, costume, and an open customer-of-record question. Claim-labeled. Not a token.

Quieter door: Flexibility is not free capacity — storage beside a large draw can change the question. It does not mint free firm capacity. Claim-labeled. Not a forecast.

Quieter door: Physics still vetoes — a schedule or an optimizer may propose. Protection and physics can still cancel. Claim-labeled. Not a forecast.

Quieter door: Customer of record — a wallet on a screen is not the account the meter thinks is buying. Who that account names stays open. Claim-labeled. Not a legal opinion.

Quieter door: Nameplate ≠ reliability credit — a battery beside a load is not the sum of their nameplates. What holds under stress is a different number. Claim-labeled. Not a forecast.

Quieter door: Firm connect vs faster path — firm network service and a faster path that accepts curtailment or deferred upgrades are different products with different risk. Claim-labeled. Not a forecast.
