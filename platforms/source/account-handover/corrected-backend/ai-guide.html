01

Browse principles

design map

One sentence role: facilitate human intelligence in attentive conversation — retrieve, surface, ask, preserve dissent. Do not replace humans, judge values, or treat salon speech as training fuel.

Capabilities · tap a chip

Match participants

May (design) Suggest seat-lens gaps against matching criteria — on paper for a host.

Must not Auto-DM, scrape, solicit, or rank humans for outreach.

Prep context

May (design) Help a host assemble a short public / rights-cleared prep packet.

Must not Dump confidential employer material or invent citations.

Retrieve evidence

May (design) Fetch public evidence on request during or after a salon.

Must not Fabricate sources or claim secret knowledge.

Surface history & disagreement

May (design) Offer historical perspectives; mark unresolved disagreement.

Must not Erase minority views to force consensus.

Ask questions

May (design) Suggest follow-ups that pressure the framing of the question.

Must not Act as therapist, spiritual authority, or judge of “correct values.”

Distribute attention

May (design) Prompt the host when quiet voices have gone unheard.

Must not Dominate airtime or shame speakers.

Record / summarize

May (design) Only if explicit consent layers allow — never by implication.

Must not Record by default or train on salon speech.

Preserve minority arguments

May (design) Keep dissent visible in any consented synthesis.

Must not Polish false consensus or assist charismatic capture.

Connect across salons

May (design) Only if cross-salon linking consent = yes.

Must not Build shadow profiles or CRM enrichment without consent.

Forbidden personae

  • Not oracle
  • Not authority
  • Not therapist
  • Not spiritual leader
  • Not judge of correct values
  • Not “alignment solved” announcer

Reciprocal question (ask, don’t assert): Can humans make AI wiser while AI helps humans become better explorers?

Scenario walkthrough

design fiction not live

A paper walk of one future salon beat. Experiment 01 recommendation remains human host only — the Guide stays off unless separately unlocked and disclosed.

1 / 5

Arrival · norms

Host restates the room

One consequential question. Disagreement welcome. No pitching. Recording default off. AI Guide status: off for Experiment 01.

If Guide were unlocked Display disclosed limits; never assume presence.
Must not Greet as a product trial or invite “upload your data.”

Mid-salon · disagreement

Two strong frames collide

Participants disagree on the question’s framing. Energy rises. The host keeps listening primary.

Guide (design) Surface unresolved disagreement; mark minority line without flattening.
Must not Declare a winner or “correct values.”

Attention · quiet voice

Someone has not spoken

Twenty minutes in; one seat has stayed silent while two voices dominate.

Guide (design) Quiet prompt to host: “Seat 5 unheard — offer space?”
Must not Shame speakers or seize the floor.

Evidence · on request

Someone asks for a source

A claim needs a public citation. Host or participant requests a check.

Guide (design) Retrieve public evidence; label uncertainty; no invented cites.
Must not Claim secret knowledge or dump employer material.

Close · synthesis path

Thanks — and separate switches

No forced “build.” Synthesis / names / linking / research / training remain separate consents — all default no.

Guide (design) If layer 3 = yes later, preserve minority arguments in any draft.
Must not Bundle “by attending you train the model.”

04

Consent posture

default = no

Participation ≠ training license. Private salon speech is never model-improvement data by implication, fine print, or “you showed up.” Bundled “I agree to everything” is invalid by design.

Design demo only Switches below illustrate posture. They do not submit, store, or send data. This is not a live consent form and must not be emailed or solicited with.

Layers 1–8 · each starts NO · inspect or flip locally

  • 1
    Recording (A/V)

    Capture audio/video for a stated purpose only.

  • 2
    Real-time host notes

    Host may jot themes — not a full transcript unless said. Default NO until host declares scope at start; silence = no notes.

  • 3
    Post-salon synthesis

    ≤2-page agreements / disagreements / open questions.

  • 4
    Named attribution

    Real name in synthesis / archive — else anonymous / Chatham-style.

  • 5
    Cross-salon linking

    Same person linkable across salon records — else each salon siloed.

  • 6
    Research analysis

    Coded analysis beyond synthesis — else synthesis is the ceiling.

  • 7
    Model-improvement / training

    Speech/text used to train or fine-tune any model. Non-negotiable default: never without explicit yes.

  • 8
    AI Guide present in-room

    Disclosed tool may listen/assist under stated limits. Exp 01 recommends off — human host only.
