Physics still vetoes

established current hypothesis open

A profitable plan can still trip. Automation proposes; protection and physics can cancel. A schedule, a price, or an optimizer’s preferred move is not the same sentence as delivery. This page stays on that cut. It does not count trips, name a control room, or forecast who wins.

Propose ≠ cleared

established current Automation, and price or schedule intent, may propose a plan. Physics and protection can cancel it. That is established as current ops and exhibition framing — the same Optimize line already on Power as a Primitive. “Optimized,” “scheduled,” and “priced” describe intent. They do not, by themselves, describe a plan that ran. This note did not measure a fleet. It names no region, no megawatts, and no trip count.

Where the veto lives

established Economic intent meets kinematics, safety envelopes, and protective relays. Ordinary practice, restated: a plan can look profitable and still be the plan a limit, a relay, or the motion of the machine will not allow. The same sentence is already exhibition framing on the Power essay and the robot-bill note. Schematic only — words for where a veto can live. Not a control-room screen, and not a procedure copied from a desk.

hypothesis Autonomy that ignores the veto is not sophistication — it is a contingency waiting to happen. “Waiting” is the claim. Not a date. Not a named outage. Not a prophecy that the contingency arrives.

Storage buffers; it does not abolish the grid

established Storage buffers autonomy. It does not abolish the grid, and it does not erase protection. Established as SoE ops intuition in exhibition copy — not a peer-reviewed finding claimed here. On Power as a Primitive, Store already says buffers change response speed and do not erase the system. The companion flexibility note points a related caution at interconnection: flex is not free firm capacity. This page stays on the veto. A buffer can change how fast a site answers. It does not retire the question of whether the plan is allowed to run.

hypothesis Intelligence that cannot respect vetoes on power is more exposed, not more autonomous. The Power essay states a wider line — locate, price, acquire, store, or respect vetoes — as the exhibition’s philosophical posture, not proven metaphysics. This note keeps the narrower cut, and labels it hypothesis. Curiosity over prophecy.

No timetable sits under the hypothesis. No forecast of a trip, a surplus, or a year when protection becomes optional. Curiosity over prophecy includes leaving the desk’s list local.

What would make this wrong

hypothesis Humility, not a new measurement. The frame breaks if a reader treats the sentences above as destiny, or as proof a plan ran.

  • Wrong if “AI optimized the dispatch” is read as proof the plan ran. Optimized is intent. Running is a different sentence.
  • Wrong if the veto is sold as optional, or as something a more sophisticated optimizer can retire.
  • Wrong if “more exposed” is read as destiny — a slogan that physics always prevails, or that automation always prevails. Neither slogan is a claim on this page.
  • Wrong if “storage buffers” is read as “storage never helps,” or as “the grid has been abolished.” The intuition says a buffer changes response. It does not erase protection.
  • Wrong if the contingency hypothesis is read as a dated prediction. It names a failure mode of ignoring the veto. It does not schedule one.
  • Wrong if a reader takes a megawatt, a market statistic, a trip count, or a control-room screen from this page. None are stated.

That humility beat is the one EXPLORE marks as what could make a frame wrong. Beat 02 on that page is what is already true. This note tries to stay inside that pair — curiosity, not prophecy.
