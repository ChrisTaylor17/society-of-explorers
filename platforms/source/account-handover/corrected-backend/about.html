About

About

Society of Explorers is an independent, noncommercial intellectual exhibition. It explores how intelligence, energy, machines, identity, ownership, markets, and coordination may converge — especially as non-human economic agents become conceivable — inside a larger question about civilization and human significance.

Why wonder The future isn’t something that merely happens to us. People participate in discovering and creating it. Individual human beings remain significant participants in whatever comes next.

What SoE is exploring

Singularity / evolution forecast Kurzweil as forecast; Singularity≈punctuated equilibrium as metaphor only.
Heidegger / QCT philosophy Gestell (enframing) & standing-reserve (the world treated as orderable resource) as real counterargument. Unverified slogans not attributed.
Polanyi philosophy “The Republic of Science is a Society of Explorers” (Minerva 1962); also McEnerney 1962 / Tacit Dimension Ch. 3. Inspired ideal — not his institution.
Consciousness belief LoA / manifestation only as spiritual-personal-belief or metaphysical-hypothesis — never physics / neuroscience.
