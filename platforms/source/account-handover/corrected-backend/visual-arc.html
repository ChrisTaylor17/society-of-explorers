Visual arc

Curiosity path: walk eight transitions as an exploratory journey — wonder first, pressure-test second. Destiny is not on the map, and this frame is not an expertise credential.

Human message The future isn’t something that merely happens to us. People participate in discovering and creating it. Individual human beings remain significant participants in whatever comes next.

Voice

Curiosity context only — not credentials. Ongoing interest in how civilization-scale questions meet energy practice and machine-economy pressure-tests. No academic degrees, professorships, or expertise claims. Voice = practitioner-explorer asking questions: wonder, intellectual seriousness, humility.
