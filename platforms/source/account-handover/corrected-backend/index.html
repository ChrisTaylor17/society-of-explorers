Society of Explorers · exhibition

An independent noncommercial intellectual exhibition exploring energy, machines, markets, and civilization.

What happens when intelligent machines can participate directly in economic systems?

Civilization is becoming more computational — but computation remains physical. Energy is a binding constraint. An enormous future may be worth participating in — not a destiny already written.

Curiosity under pressure: could non-human agents use some economic primitives — identity, contracts, wallets, reputation, energy, physical agency — and what follows for markets and coordination?
